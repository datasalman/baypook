# Decisions the spec left open

Each entry: what was open, what BayPook does, why. Values that could change are editable in the admin.

1. **Env var names for per-venue Stripe keys.** Slugs contain hyphens, which env var names cannot. The suffix is the slug upper-cased with any non-alphanumeric run replaced by `_`: `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY__SOUTH_WOODFORD`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET__LAKESIDE`. The Connections page prints the exact name to set.
2. **Hold length, reminder lead and retention live on the organisation row** (Settings), not in a key/value table, so they have labelled fields and defaults (15 min, 24 h, 24 months). A `settings` key/value table exists for anything else.
3. **Opening hours are stored on the venue as JSON** (`{ mon: null | { open, close }, … }`). Timetable rules for sessions are explicit per weekday and are not gated by opening hours (the owner's rules win); slot starts are gated by opening hours. Seeded hours are flagged `openingHoursConfirmed = false` and shown as "confirm" in Settings.
4. **Session materialisation reconciles rather than regenerates.** `ensureSessions` inserts missing occurrences, updates unpinned rows that drifted (capacity, room, length, status), and handles rows the rules no longer produce: deleted when nothing references them; set `cancelled` (unpinned, so a restored rule brings them back) when only expired holds or cancelled bookings reference them; pinned as manual and kept scheduled when they still have live bookings. Admin edits to a single occurrence set `pinned = true` so later generation leaves it alone.
5. **In-store notes exist on both services and options.** The Decoden workshop note belongs to the option (one service, two options); a Decoden party's note belongs to the service. The booking page shows option notes beside the option and service notes under the total.
6. **Roles.** `users.isOwner` grants everything everywhere; non-owners have `user_venues` rows with `manager` or `staff`. Managers can refund; staff cannot. Refund permission is checked server-side in the refund action.
7. **Demo users.** `owner@demo.baypook` (owner), `manager.southwoodford@demo.baypook` (manager, South Woodford), `staff.lakeside@demo.baypook` (staff, Lakeside). The demo login page offers all three so venue scoping can be rehearsed.
8. **Live mode without a provider.** Email and calendar fall back to the demo adapter with a warning banner in the admin (nothing is dropped silently; it lands in the Outbox / Calendar log). Payments do not fall back: a venue with no Stripe key is not bookable online and `/api/v1` returns `UNAVAILABLE` for it.
9. **Google Calendar via REST + `google-auth-library`** rather than the full `googleapis` package (smaller, same API).
10. **Migrations run at runtime only in demo** (PGlite). In live mode `npm run db:migrate` (drizzle-kit) is part of the deploy command, so a cold serverless function never migrates.
11. **Payment status on bookings** is a denormalised summary (`unpaid | owed | paid | refunded | partially_refunded`) kept in step with the `payments` and `refunds` ledger, so lists and reports do not need joins.
12. **New services start with online booking off** so a service with no options or times cannot be booked by accident; the flash message says to switch it on.
13. **A new venue starts "closed"** with one "Main room" and unconfirmed 10:00–18:00 hours every day.
14. **Placeholder flags** clear when the field is saved with a new value or its "This is correct" box is ticked; terms and waiver flags clear when the wording changes; the organisation's "openingHours" flag clears once every venue's hours are confirmed.
15. **Terms and waiver are versioned separately**, each with "Save as new version" and "Save wording only". Bookings record the versions they accepted.
16. **Admin entry units:** notice in hours, cut-off in minutes, prices in pounds (stored in pence).
17. **A single session's capacity cannot go below the places already booked.** Adding a block warns how many bookings overlap; a "cancel" exception reports the sessions it kept because they have bookings. Neither cancels bookings: staff move or cancel them one by one, audited.
18. **Rooms cannot be deleted** while services, sessions, bookings, holds or blocks reference them, or when they are the venue's last room.
19. **Reports:** a payment counts on the day it was taken; a refund is subtracted on the day it was given; imported Wix payments are left out of takings; CSV cells starting with `= + - @` are prefixed with an apostrophe to stop spreadsheet formula injection.
20. **Users:** nobody can change their own role or deactivate themselves; the last active owner cannot be demoted or deactivated; deactivating deletes the user's sessions. Users are never deleted.
21. **Walk-ins without an email** are stored with a blank email (a shared placeholder would merge every email-less walk-in into one customer). No customer email is sent for them on booking, move, cancel or refund; the owner alert and calendar push still happen. The manual-booking wizard can pick an existing customer by id so repeat walk-ins are not duplicated.
22. **Demo users exist only in demo mode.** A live database gets its first owner from `scripts/seed.ts --owner you@…`; no `@demo.baypook` accounts are created there.
23. **Sign-in links are not stored in live mode.** The Outbox keeps a redacted body for `magic_link` emails (the link is only in the recipient's inbox); demo mode keeps the full link so the owner can click it from the Outbox.
24. **Security headers** are set for every route: `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN` and `frame-ancestors 'self'` (not DENY, because the Outbox and template previews are same-origin srcdoc iframes), `nosniff`, a strict referrer policy, a restrictive permissions policy and HSTS in production.
25. **Exports of customer details and takings** need an owner or a manager of every venue in scope; staff can export the bookings list for their venue only.
26. **Payment amount check.** A Stripe event confirms a booking only when the amount paid is at least the booking total in the organisation currency; anything else is left pending and the owner is emailed.
27. **Hold hoarding is capped per client.** `POST /api/v1/holds` needs no sign-in, so it is limited by client, where the client is an HMAC of its IP keyed with `APP_SECRET` (raw IPs are never stored). A client can have at most 3 holds that keep places at once (active, unexpired and not yet handed to checkout); a 4th is refused with `LIMIT` (`limit: 3`, "You already have 3 bookings on hold. Finish or wait for them to run out."), and releasing a hold, checking out or letting one run out frees a slot. On top of the light in-memory limit per instance (120 reads or 20 writes a minute), a shared fixed-window limit in the database (`rate_limits`, one atomic upsert per request) allows 10 holds, 10 checkouts and 60 quotes a minute per client across all instances; a refusal is `RATE_LIMITED` with `Retry-After`. The daily retention job deletes `rate_limits` rows older than a day. Trade-offs: people behind one shared IP (a school or office network) share the cap, and the client IP comes from `x-forwarded-for`, which is trustworthy only behind a proxy that sets it (Vercel does); a Vercel WAF rule on `/api/v1/holds` and `/api/v1/checkout` (SETUP.md step 6) remains a sensible extra layer.
28. **Refunds are reserved before the card is refunded.** `refundBooking` locks the booking, re-checks what is refundable, inserts a `pending` refund row that already counts in `refundedPence`, then calls the provider with that row's id as the idempotency key and settles the row; a failed refund is reversed. Two simultaneous refunds therefore cannot over-refund.
29. **Payment status rule: owed means `total − paid > 0`.** Refunds never make a booking owe again. A booking that is not cancelled and has `total − paid > 0` is `owed` (or `unpaid` for an online booking with nothing paid and nothing refunded); otherwise anything refunded makes it `refunded` (all of it) or `partially_refunded`; otherwise it is `paid`. So a goodwill partial refund with no change of places is `partially_refunded`, not owed, while places added after a partial refund are owed as soon as the new total is above what was paid. The owed figure is `total − paid` everywhere it is shown: the Outstanding report (which lists confirmed and no-show bookings with `total − paid > 0`), the booking page, the payment line in emails and calendar events, the thanks page and the change-places page. When a change of places leaves the total below `paid − refunded`, the admin is offered a refund of the difference. Cancelled bookings never show as owing.
30. **Prices already paid are kept.** Changing counts keeps the unit price of places already sold and prices only the added places (or new add-ons) from today's catalogue; reductions drop the newest-priced places first. One option can therefore appear on two lines of a booking.
31. **Leftover sessions are never deleted while anything references them.** When the timetable no longer produces a session that has any booking or hold row, the row is kept: scheduled and pinned if it has live bookings (pending, confirmed, no-show), otherwise cancelled and unpinned (a session held only by a hold is cancelled, and that hold's checkout is refused with `GONE`). If the rules produce it again later, the unpinned row is restored.
32. **Superseded checkouts.** A second checkout on the same hold cancels the earlier pending booking; if the earlier one is paid anyway, the newer pending booking is cancelled in the same transaction after a capacity re-check, and its payment page is closed.
